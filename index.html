I am a researcher and engineer from Da Nang, Vietnam. My background is in algorithms and data structures, which I've worked on since high school competitive programming at Le Quy Don High School for the Gifted, Da Nang. I currently work on LayerProof, which builds AI tools that turn source material into presentations and reports, with every claim tied back to its sources. I also contribute to LQDOJ, an open-source online judge for Vietnamese students, where I write articles and set contest problems.

Publications

A Hybrid Genetic Algorithm for the Steiner Tree Problem in Graphs towards Optimizations for Wireless Sensor Networks
Tang Phu Quy Le, Bao Nhan Ho Sy, Tan Phu Quoc Hoang, Cong Phap Huynh, Dai Tho Dang
The University of Danang – Journal of Science and Technology, Vol. 22, No. 6B, 2024
paper / code

A hybrid genetic algorithm (HGA) for the Steiner tree problem in graphs, motivated by node placement in wireless sensor networks. Population diversity is maintained with a 2-longest-distance selection strategy, a dynamic crossover rate and a minimum-difference rule between chosen solutions; evaluated on SteinLib benchmarks.
