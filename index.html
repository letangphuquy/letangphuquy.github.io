Research

I'm interested in how learning can make optimization better: neural combinatorial optimization, automatic heuristic design, and evolutionary and multi-objective metaheuristics. On the applied side, I like turning real-world problems — transport networks, facility location, disaster response — into rigorous mathematical models and designing algorithms that outperform existing approaches on them.

A Hybrid Genetic Algorithm for the Steiner Tree Problem in Graphs towards Optimizations for Wireless Sensor Networks
Tang Phu Quy Le, Bao Nhan Ho Sy, Tan Phu Quoc Hoang, Cong Phap Huynh, Dai Tho Dang
The University of Danang – Journal of Science and Technology, Vol. 22, No. 6B, 2024
paper / code

A hybrid genetic algorithm for the Steiner tree problem in graphs, motivated by node placement in wireless sensor networks. Diversity is maintained with a 2-longest-distance selection strategy, a dynamic crossover rate and a minimum-difference rule between selected solutions; evaluated on SteinLib benchmarks.
